# Alzheimer phenotype maps

These six maps come from NeuroVault collection
[8546](https://identifiers.org/neurovault.collection:8546), associated with:

La Joie R, Visani AV, Lesman-Segev OH, et al. Association of APOE4 and Clinical
Variability in Alzheimer Disease With the Pattern of Tau- and Amyloid-PET.
*Neurology*. 2021;96:e650-e661. DOI: `10.1212/WNL.0000000000011270`.

The study included 69 patients with typical amnestic Alzheimer disease, 21 with
posterior cortical atrophy, and 29 with logopenic primary progressive aphasia.
All were amyloid-positive. The unthresholded tau-PET contrasts control for age,
CDR-SB, and global tau SUVR. The VBM contrasts control for age, CDR-SB, and
global cortical gray-matter volume.
